Bloxorz Unblocked

By TotallyScience.co

Bloxorz Unblocked is a 3D puzzle game developed with Flash technology. In the game, your objective is to direct a dark brown block and get it into the square hole. However, you can't let it fall from the edges!

To steer the block well, you will need to stretch your brain. There are 33 levels, and each gets trickier as you progress. Strive to overcome all the levels in this brain game! Let's see how far you can go.

Guide the Block to the Hole

  • When you enter the game, your block will be on tiles chained together.
  • Your job is to navigate your block to the square hole by using the WASD or the arrow keys.
  • You must ensure that it enters the hole in a way that passes right through the square hole.
  • While trying to do that, you shouldn't let it fall off the edges. Otherwise, you must restart from the beginning.
  • Additionally, you will come across many switches and bridges in later levels.
  • You will need to use the switches to your advantage to make progress.

Three Types of Switches

  • There are three types of switches to use in this brain game.
  • The first one is the Heavy X-shaped switches. You need to create pressure by standing on them with your cube to activate them.
  • On the other hand, Soft switches do not require this kind of pressure. A light touch is enough to activate them.
  • The third type of switch is in the ( ) shape. It will enable you to teleport to a different location.
  • Parentheses switches will split your block into two smaller pieces. Use your spacebar to choose one of the pieces and guide it.
  • When they get close to each other, they will become whole again.
