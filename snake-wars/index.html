Snake Wars Unblocked

By TotallyScience.co

Snake Wars Unblocked is a 2D io game crafted with Unity. In the game, you will guide a little snake on a pit. You must feed your animal with the neon foods scattered around the field.

As you feed it, it will grow and get bigger in size. Dodge other online players who are also trying to hunt for food. If you collide with them, you must start from the beginning in this arcade game!

Controls

Desktops and Laptops

  • Mouse: Steer the Animal
  • Left-Click or Spacebar: Activate Booster

Mobile Devices

  • Use the touchscreen to steer the animal.
  • Tap on the virtual button to activate the booster.

Grow Your Snake!

As you do in every single snake game, your main objective is to steer your animal in a pit with many foods. Collect the foods to grow your little animal and watch it get bigger in size.

The score will increase as you collect the food pellets. You will have 2 minutes and 30 seconds. Try to get the most nourishment in this short period to be the leading snake on the leaderboard.
