What is drifting?

  • As you can guess from the Fast and Furious series, one of the popular drift movies, this motorsport technique was popularized in Japan in the 1970s.
  • So, how is drift performed? The drivers intentionally oversteer their cars to make them slip on the road by using the brakes.
  • In order to perform the best drift, it is of great importance to reach a certain speed and use the brakes at the most appropriate time.
  • When the driver steers the wheel, the car starts to slide and turn. Maintaining control of the vehicle is necessary to ensure the continuation of this long turn.
  • However, this driving technique has its dangers. Since it requires high speed and sudden maneuvers, the driver has a high probability of having an accident.
  • That's exactly why the games in the drift games unblocked category offers you the chance to show your best drift performance in the virtual environment without worrying about having an accident.
