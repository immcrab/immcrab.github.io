What does io mean in games?

Actually, it is a domain extension name which stands for “Indian Ocean”. It belongs to the British Indian Ocean territory. However, this territory has nothing to do with the io games category!

This category stems from the Agar.io game, which is the first game of this genre. When this game gained serious popularity in the multiplayer field with its domain, we can say that a genre called io games emerged.

Let’s Talk About io Games

What is an io game? Basically, they are browser games with multiplayer options. They unite people from all over the world in one place. This way, all online players can play the same game at the same time. Also, they are created with very simple game mechanics so that anyone can play them. Most of them can be played often using just a mouse.

For example, think about unblocked grow games. The main objective of this kind of entertaining games is to grow your territory. You must claim your lands and protect them. It's very simple, isn't it?

Possess the most extensive territory on the white arena to be the top player! Of course, you should be careful not to come into contact with other cubes while doing this. Otherwise, all your efforts will be in vain and you will be back to square one.

Purpose of Unblocked io Games

We can say that the common purpose of all the games in this genre is that playing with online players worldwide in real time. But not only that. The theme of survival is also a frequently encountered issue in these games.

Additionally, making something bigger may also be among the objectives of this genre just as you do in Snake io Unblocked! You strive to grow the size of your cute snake on a pit by collecting the food pellets. As it gets food, it becomes bigger and bigger. Moreover, this animal must escape from other snakes guided by online players to survive.

Everyone against everyone!

Most of the time, it is everyone against everyone in a multiplayer arena in these games. When you take a look at Dont Fall io, you will see that there are many platforms with falling hexagons. And all the players try to move forward and backward on them, trying to survive.

To be the winner, you must stand still until each player falls to the sea. So, you need to jump from one hexagon piece to another without staying too long. Otherwise, you may be the one who sinks in the water!
