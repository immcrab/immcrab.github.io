What are Minecraft Games?

Minecraft unblocked games are sandbox games inspired by the popular video game Minecraft. They include crafting, surviving, and exploring.

Common Controls

Even though there are slight changes in inventory and game modes, the following controls are common in most Unblocked Minecraft Games.

  • Move: WASD
  • Jump/Fly: Space
  • Break Blocks: Left Click
  • Place Blocks: Right Click

Common Game Modes

Minecraft games have three main game modes. Some games may have less or more than these three modes: survival, peaceful and building. 

  • Survival is a mode in which players defend themselves from wild nature by building a shelter and finding food and water.
  • Peaceful is a mode where players explore and discover crafting islands without worrying about survival. Dig, carve, collect resources and explore all over. 
  • Creative is a building mode that provides you with all the blocks available in the game. The maps are often flat in this mode to facilitate building. Build your dreamland, a fortress, a skyscraper or a whole new town.

Basic Crafting Recipes

The best part of the Minecraft games is exploring new recipes. After collecting resources, open the crafting table and drag wood, stones, grass, or other objects to create new materials. We're giving you the basic Minecraft recipes below and, you're free to explore more yourself or search for recipes online.

  • Planks: Drag wood blocks
  • Sticks: Drag planks
  • Axes: Unite sticks and mines in T shape
  • Ladder: Unite sticks
  • Fence: Combine sticks with planks of any wood type
  • Bread: Craft 3 piles of grass
